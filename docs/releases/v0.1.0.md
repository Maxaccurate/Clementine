## ZestDrop v0.1.0

This release adds an English interface, lets you pause, cancel or hide each conversion, runs two jobs at once, and fixes several reliability problems.

Earlier releases were renumbered: the former v0.1.0 is now **v0.0.1** and the former v0.1.1 is now **v0.0.2**.

### What's new

- **English interface.** Choose English or Simplified Chinese from the tray menu (Language / 语言). The first launch follows your Windows display language, and the choice is saved. Error messages from the conversion engine follow the same language.
- **Pause, cancel and hide each job.** Every background job gets its own progress card:
  - **Pause / Resume** freezes the whole job, including FFmpeg and other helper programs, and stops the elapsed-time clock. Resume continues exactly where it stopped.
  - **Cancel** stops the job and removes its unfinished files. Paused jobs can be cancelled directly.
  - **Hide** tucks the card away while the job keeps running. A tray notification reports when a hidden job finishes.
- **Several jobs at once.** Two jobs run in parallel; further jobs wait their turn and are shown as waiting. Pausing a job lets the next one start. Cards are listed top to bottom in the order they started. With two or more jobs, a header shows the counts and can **Collapse** the cards into a pile, **Expand** them again, or **Hide all**. The tray menu can show progress, pause or resume all jobs, and cancel all jobs.
- **No more 30-minute limit.** Long video encodes are no longer stopped after 30 minutes; cancel them when you want instead.

### Fixes

- Conversions could occasionally fail with "处理引擎未正常完成" (the engine did not finish properly), or skip the rest of a batch, even though the files had converted. This happened when the progress display read the progress file at the moment it was being updated.
- Cancelling a job, or quitting during one, no longer leaves half-written `.zestdrop-…` files next to your originals. Cancelling one job never touches another job's files in the same folder.
- F8 and F9 are no longer taken from other applications. They open the menu only while you are dragging files, so Excel recalculation and debugger shortcuts work normally again.
- Plain `.gz` files and RAR archives now get the same size and file-count limits as ZIP and TAR when extracting.
- Very large PDF pages are rendered at a reduced resolution instead of running out of memory.
- Logs and temporary previews moved to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\ZestDrop\logs`. The log is rotated at 4 MB, no longer records the full paths of your files, and an unwritable application folder can no longer stop the app from starting.

### Download and upgrade

Download `ZestDrop-v0.1.0-windows-x64.zip`, extract the entire archive, and run `ZestDrop.exe` from the `ZestDrop` folder. Keep its `backend`, `runtime`, and DLLs together. The bundle includes .NET, Python, FFmpeg, 7-Zip, and the required Python libraries.

Before upgrading, finish or cancel current jobs and quit the old application from its tray menu. Extract this version into a fresh directory and launch it. A SHA-256 checksum is attached.

### Requirements and validation

- Windows x64. Interface in English or Simplified Chinese.
- Office layout exports require the corresponding desktop Microsoft PowerPoint, Word, or Excel application.
- The executable is unsigned. There is no installer, auto-update, or startup registration.
- Automated checks cover the backend conversions, tool parameters, the progress stack, pausing a real FFmpeg encode and resuming it to a valid file, cleanup after cancelling, and that every interface string has an English translation. Office conversions were not re-tested for this release, and multi-monitor and display-scaling setups still need broader testing.
- Existing PDF/OCR and multi-frame image limitations continue to apply; see the [usage guide](https://github.com/Maxaccurate/ZestDrop/blob/main/docs/usage.md).

Report reproducible bugs in [Issues](https://github.com/Maxaccurate/ZestDrop/issues). Please avoid uploading private input files.
